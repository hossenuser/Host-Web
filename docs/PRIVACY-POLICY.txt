MH APPS GALLERY – PRIVACY NOTICE

This starter application loads publicly available posts from https://mhappsgallery.blogspot.com/.
It stores only your display preferences (theme) and saved favourite post identifiers locally in
this browser/app profile. This starter kit does not include user accounts, analytics, advertising
SDKs, or a custom notification server.

When you open a post or the official website, the destination website may process data under its
own privacy policy. The app cannot guarantee the availability or safety of third-party download
links; check the publisher and file before installing downloaded software.

Push notifications are not active in this starter build. They require a notification provider,
server-side credentials, a privacy notice update, and platform permission. Never place private
server keys inside the app source.
